Frequently Asked Questions

Booking kaise kare?

Aap hamari website ke booking form se, ya seedha WhatsApp/Call karke instant booking kar sakte hain.

Payment ke options kya hain?

Hum UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Cash, aur delivery ke waqt payment jaisi sabhi convenient options accept karte hain.

Driver kab assign hota hai?

Booking submit aur confirm hone ke turant baad hamara verified driver assign kar diya jaata hai jiska contact detail aapko mil jaata hai.

Haan, hum local (Dhamtari, Raipur, Durg) aur interstate long-distance dono tarah ki safe freight transportation service provide karte hain.
